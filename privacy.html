Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Battery Life ("the app") is made by ezlong. This policy explains what the app uses and why. The app has no accounts and no sign-up. From version 1.2 the free version shows ads; buying "Go ad-free" removes them.

What the app uses

  • Battery information (level, charging state, and on Android the charging current, voltage and temperature) is read from your device to show charging speed and time to full. It never leaves your device.
  • Location (latitude and longitude, only if you allow it) is sent to our weather server to get the weather where you are and to look up the place name. It is not stored on our server and is not linked to you.
  • Settings such as your music and background choices are saved only on your device.

What the app does not collect

  • No name, email, phone number or account.
  • We do not run our own analytics and do not sell your information.
  • No contacts, photos or files.

Services the app connects to

  • Weather: Visual Crossing, through our server on Cloudflare.
  • Place names: OpenStreetMap Nominatim.
  • Music and background photos: Cloudflare R2 and Google Firebase Storage (photos from Pexels and Wikimedia Commons).
  • These services receive only what is needed to answer each request (for example, your approximate location for the weather).

Google Photos (optional)

  • If you choose "Import from Google Photos", the app asks for one Google permission: photospicker.mediaitems.readonly (view the photos and videos you select in Google Photos).
  • The app can see only the items you pick in the Google Photos picker. It cannot browse, change or delete your library.
  • The selected photos are downloaded directly from Google to your device and shown in the photo frame. They are not sent to our servers or to anyone else, and are not used for ads or to train AI models.
  • The sign-in token is kept only on your device (iPhone Keychain or Android account services). You can remove imported photos in the app, and revoke access anytime at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Battery Life's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Advertising (free version)

  • Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iPhone only if you allow tracking, Android advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from it, app interaction and ad performance data, and crash diagnostics.
  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent first (Google User Messaging Platform). You can change your choice anytime in Settings > Privacy settings.
  • On iPhone the app asks permission to track (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalized and the IDFA is not used.
  • How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Purchases

  • "Go ad-free" (monthly, yearly or lifetime) is sold through the App Store or Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google; we never see your card or account details. The app only checks with the store whether you own the purchase.

Children

  • The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

  • If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Para exibir e medir anúncios, o SDK de anúncios do Google pode coletar o identificador de publicidade do aparelho (IDFA no iPhone só se você permitir o rastreamento, ID de publicidade no Android), o endereço IP e a localização aproximada derivada dele, dados de interação com o app e de desempenho dos anúncios, e diagnósticos de falhas.
  • No Espaço Econômico Europeu, no Reino Unido e na Suíça o app pede seu consentimento primeiro (Google User Messaging Platform). Você pode mudar a escolha quando quiser em Ajustes > Configurações de privacidade.
  • No iPhone o app pede permissão para rastreamento (App Tracking Transparency). Se você recusar, os anúncios continuam, mas sem personalização e sem usar o IDFA.
  • Como o Google usa os dados: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Compras

  • "Sem anúncios" (mensal, anual ou vitalício) é vendido pela App Store ou pelo Google Play. O pagamento é feito inteiramente pela Apple ou pelo Google; nunca vemos seu cartão ou sua conta. O app só verifica com a loja se você tem a compra.

Crianças

  • O app não é direcionado a menores de 13 anos e não coleta intencionalmente dados pessoais deles.

Alterações
