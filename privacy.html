Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Battery Life ("the app") is made by ezlong. This policy explains what the app uses and why. The app has no accounts, no sign-up, no ads and no tracking.

What the app uses

  • Battery information (level, charging state, and on Android the charging current, voltage and temperature) is read from your device to show charging speed and time to full. It never leaves your device.
  • Location (latitude and longitude, only if you allow it) is sent to our weather server to get the weather where you are and to look up the place name. It is not stored on our server and is not linked to you.
  • Settings such as your music and background choices are saved only on your device.

What the app does not collect

  • No name, email, phone number or account.
  • No advertising identifier and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No contacts, photos or files.

Services the app connects to

  • Weather: Visual Crossing, through our server on Cloudflare.
  • Place names: OpenStreetMap Nominatim.
  • Music and background photos: Cloudflare R2 and Google Firebase Storage (photos from Pexels and Wikimedia Commons).
  • These services receive only what is needed to answer each request (for example, your approximate location for the weather).

Children

  • The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

  • If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
